🛢️ Pickups & Light Trucks — Under 1 Tonne

Diesel Vehicles • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

The diesel pickup war is Vietnam's toughest contest. All four Japanese/US nameplates are durable; they differ in economy (D-Max wins), comfort (Ranger wins) and toughness reputation (Hilux wins). Every one starts instantly and pulls from idle — the diesel smoke of old pickups is gone in these Euro 5 models.

🏆 Ranked Vehicles — Best First

#1Isuzu D-Max 1.9

Japan — imported (Thailand)
⚡ Consumption: ≈6.8 L/100 km combined — most frugal pickup💰 Maker price: from 665 million VND
✅ Pros
  • The 1.9 RZ4E is the economy king and famously long-lived
  • Simple, honest engineering; low fault rate in fleet use
  • Cheaper to buy and run than Ranger/Hilux
⚠️ Cons
  • Cabin and ride comfort trail Ranger
  • Engine is adequate, not exciting
  • Smaller dealer network
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (Prestige MT): steel wheels, fabric, basic audio.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (Type Z / Hi-Lander AT): leather, ADAS, 9-inch screen, diff lock.

#2Ford Ranger 2.0

USA — assembled in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈8.0 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 669 million VND
✅ Pros
  • Best-selling pickup in Vietnam — car-like cabin and tech
  • Strong Bi-Turbo option and 10-speed automatic
  • Huge accessory and resale market
⚠️ Cons
  • Uses more fuel than D-Max/Hilux in real traffic
  • Electronics need dealer updates; parts pricier
  • Big dimensions for old streets
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (XL MT): steel wheels, fabric seats, small screen.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (Wildtrak / Stormtrak / Raptor): matrix LED, 12-inch screen, ADAS, premium trim (Raptor: Fox suspension).

#3Toyota Hilux 2.4 / 2.8

Japan — imported (Thailand)
⚡ Consumption: ≈7.8 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 668 million VND
✅ Pros
  • The global symbol of the unbreakable pickup
  • Superb resale; parts everywhere on Earth
  • 2.8L version pulls heavy loads with ease
⚠️ Cons
  • Ride is stiff when the bed is empty
  • Cabin tech is conservative
  • Price premium over Triton/D-Max
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (2.4 MT 4x2): steel wheels, vinyl/fabric.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (2.8 Adventure 4x4): leather, JBL, Safety Sense, 360° camera.

#4Mitsubishi Triton 2.4

Japan — imported (Thailand)
⚡ Consumption: ≈7.9 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 655 million VND

⭐ Editor's Verdict

Economy champion: Isuzu D-Max 1.9. Best all-round: Ford Ranger. Toughest reputation: Toyota Hilux. Best value newcomer: Mitsubishi Triton Athlete.

Which model ranks first in this class, and why?
