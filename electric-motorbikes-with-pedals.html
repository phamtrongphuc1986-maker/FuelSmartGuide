🚲 Electric Motorbikes With Pedals

Pedal Electric Two-Wheelers • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

Electric motorbikes with pedals are Vietnam's student and short-trip staple: cheap to buy, cheap to run, park anywhere, and the pedals are a legal and practical backup when the battery runs dry. Yadea — the world's largest maker — leads the ranking.

🏆 Ranked Vehicles — Best First

#1Yadea E3

China — sold nationwide in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈70–90 km per charge (lead-acid/LFP versions)💰 Maker price: from 15.9 million VND
✅ Pros
  • World No.1 electric two-wheeler brand; dealers in every province
  • Removable battery versions charge anywhere
  • Pedals fitted — legal backup and exercise option
⚠️ Cons
  • Lead-acid versions are heavy and age faster
  • Top speed limited to about 40 km/h
  • Basic suspension on rough roads
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (graphene lead-acid): standard battery, drum brakes.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (lithium/LFP): lighter removable lithium pack, disc brake, smart display.

#2Yadea Ulike

China — sold nationwide in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈60–80 km per charge💰 Maker price: from 13.9 million VND
✅ Pros
  • Lower price with the same dealer network
  • Light and friendly for students and new riders
  • Pedal-equipped, simple to maintain
⚠️ Cons
  • Smaller battery — shorter range
  • Less weather protection
  • Basic instrumentation
Base version (bản thiếu): Base: lead-acid standard.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full: lithium version, upgraded tyres and display.
