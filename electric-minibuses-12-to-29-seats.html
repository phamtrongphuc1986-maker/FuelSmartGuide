⚡ Minibuses — 12 to Under 29 Seats

Electric Vehicles • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

Electric minibuses are the fastest-growing answer for hotels, resorts and airports: silent, smoke-free passenger loading, instant departure and tiny fuel bills. Vietnam supply is still import-based — the proven global vans are ranked below.

#1Ford E-Transit

USA — global market
⚡ Consumption: ≈28 kWh/100 km — 196 miles range (EPA)💰 Maker price: Global market — from about $45,000 (US)
✅ Pros
  • Instant, silent departures — guests love it
  • Huge cargo/passenger flexibility, proven Transit platform
  • Low maintenance: no oil, no DPF, few moving parts
