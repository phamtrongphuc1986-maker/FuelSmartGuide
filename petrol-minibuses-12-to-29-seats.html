⛽ Minibuses — 12 to Under 29 Seats

Gasoline (Petrol) Vehicles • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

Honest market note: 12–29-seat passenger transport in Vietnam is dominated by diesel (Transit, Solati) and, increasingly, electric vans. Petrol models in this class exist mainly in export markets — they are smoother, quieter and smoke-free at start-up, and suit hotels and resorts that ban diesel fumes. The proven global petrol options are ranked below.
