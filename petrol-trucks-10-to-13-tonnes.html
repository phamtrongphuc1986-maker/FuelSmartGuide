⛽ Trucks — 10 to Under 13 Tonnes

Gasoline (Petrol) Vehicles • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

There is no production petrol truck in the 10–13-tonne class anywhere in the world. The closest gas-fuel technology is CNG (compressed natural gas), used by Chinese heavy trucks in export fleets: it starts clean like petrol and costs less to run, but needs CNG stations that Vietnam barely has.
