Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 2026 • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

1. Be accurate

Share corrections and experiences honestly. Do not invent specifications, prices or ownership experiences.

2. Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech or spam in any channel connected with this website, including shared posts and feedback.

3. No commercial misuse

Dealers and sellers must not pose as independent readers to promote their stock, and must not copy our rankings into advertisements without credit.

4. Enforcement

We may ignore, remove or block contributions that breach these guidelines, and correct the record publicly where a false claim could mislead buyers.
