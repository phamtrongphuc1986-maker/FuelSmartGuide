🛢️ Trucks — 3 to Under 5 Tonnes

Diesel Vehicles • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

At 3–5 tonnes the Japanese trio — Isuzu NPR, Hino 300 and Fuso Canter — set the standard, with Hyundai's Mighty as the Korean value alternative. This is the sweet spot for construction supply, furniture and municipal fleets.

🏆 Ranked Vehicles — Best First

#1Isuzu NPR400 / NQR550

Japan — assembled in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈11 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 680 million VND
✅ Pros
  • The default 5-tonne-class truck of Vietnamese fleets
  • Excellent fuel economy and legendary engine life
  • Parts everywhere; every mechanic knows it
⚠️ Cons
  • Cab comfort is workmanlike, not plush
  • Price above Hyundai/Fuso rivals
  • Manual gearbox only on most versions
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (chassis cab): vinyl trim, steel wheels.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (factory box/tipper): insulated bodies, air-con, telematics-ready.

#2Hino XZU720 (300 Series)

Japan — assembled in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈11.5 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 700 million VND
✅ Pros
  • Smoothest, quietest drive in class; Toyota-group quality
  • Strong dealer standards and warranty support
  • Good resale value
⚠️ Cons
  • Slightly thirstier than Isuzu
  • Genuine parts are pricier
  • Fewer rural service points than Isuzu
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (chassis): standard cab.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (XZU730 wide cab): automatic option, better seats, safety pack.

#3Hyundai Mighty EX8

Korea — assembled in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈11.8 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 640 million VND
✅ Pros
  • Lower price than the Japanese pair with a roomy cab
  • Strong 3.9L D4 engine pulls well when loaded
  • Good TC Motor coverage
⚠️ Cons
  • Fuel use a touch higher
  • Long-term durability record shorter than Isuzu's
  • Resale mid-pack
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (chassis/deck): standard trim.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (EX8 GT): upgraded cab, factory bodies, longer warranty offers.

#4Fuso Canter TF

Japan — assembled in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈11.3 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 650 million VND
✅ Pros
  • Daimler-engineered; comfortable and safe cab
  • Good turning circle for city work
  • Strong global parts system
⚠️ Cons
  • Dealer network thinner than Isuzu/Hino
  • Price mid-to-high
  • Some electronic systems need dealer tools
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (Canter 4.99): standard cab.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (Canter 6.5): wide cab, automatic (Duonic) on some markets, premium trim.
