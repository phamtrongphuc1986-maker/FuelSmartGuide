⛽ Pickups & Light Trucks — Under 1 Tonne

Gasoline (Petrol) Vehicles • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

Under one tonne, petrol still rules the city: mini trucks start instantly on cold mornings, idle cleanly in alleys where diesels smoke, and cost little to buy. Vietnam's own champion is the Suzuki Carry Pro; imported petrol pickups serve buyers who want comfort with a small bed.

🏆 Ranked Vehicles — Best First

#1Suzuki Carry Pro 1.5

Japan — assembled in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈6.5 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 330 million VND
✅ Pros
  • The best-selling mini truck in Vietnam — bulletproof K15B petrol engine
  • Tiny turning circle and low loading deck, perfect for old-quarter alleys
  • Cheapest parts and maintenance of any truck on this list
⚠️ Cons
  • Payload is modest; struggles with chronic overloading
  • Very basic cab — no comfort features to speak of
  • No diesel torque for steep ramps when fully loaded
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (flat deck): open cargo bed, vinyl seats, no air-con on the lowest spec.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (box/refrigerated body): factory van or insulated box body, air-con, power steering.

#2Thaco Towner 990

Vietnam/China platform — assembled in Vietnam
⚡ Consumption: ≈6.8 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: from 270 million VND
✅ Pros
  • Lowest entry price of any new truck in Vietnam
  • Wide Thaco service network, even in small towns
  • Several body styles from factory: deck, van, tipper
⚠️ Cons
  • Chinese-sourced engine is less proven over 200,000 km than Suzuki's
  • Cabin and paint quality are a step below Japanese rivals
  • Resale value drops quickly
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (deck): basic cab, steel wheels.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (van body): enclosed box, air-con, upgraded interior trim.

#3Toyota Hilux 2.7 Petrol 4x2

Japan — global/export market
⚡ Consumption: ≈10.5 L/100 km combined💰 Maker price: Import only — price on request
✅ Pros
  • Hilux toughness with a quiet, smoke-free petrol engine
  • Comfortable double-cab for mixed family and work use
  • Outstanding global resale value
⚠️ Cons
  • Not officially sold in Vietnam — diesel Hilux dominates locally
  • Petrol torque is weaker for towing than the 2.8 diesel
  • Import parts lead time for petrol-specific items
Base version (bản thiếu): Base (WorkMate-type): steel wheels, vinyl floor, manual air-con.
Full version (bản đầy đủ): Full (SR5-type): alloy wheels, touchscreen, cruise control, chrome trim.

⭐ Editor's Verdict

For business: Suzuki Carry Pro — nothing else comes close on cost per kilometre. For family-plus-cargo use, an imported Hilux 2.7 petrol is the smooth, quiet choice.
