🚲 Electric Bicycles With Pedals

Pedal Electric Two-Wheelers • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

True electric bicycles (pedal-first, motor-assist, up to 25 km/h class) are the cheapest motorised transport in Vietnam: no fuel, no licence hassles in most cases, and healthy pedalling when you want it. Giant — the world's biggest bicycle maker — tops the list.
