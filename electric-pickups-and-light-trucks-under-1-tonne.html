⚡ Pickups & Light Trucks — Under 1 Tonne

Electric Vehicles • Ranked by brand reputation, years in service, low fault rate and fuel/energy economy • fuelsmartguide.io.vn

Electric light trucks and vans are ideal city tools: instant torque from every stop, no smoke in loading bays, and overnight charging at the depot. Vietnam now has its own champion — the VinFast EC Van — while global electric pickups show where the segment is heading.

#2Ford F-150 Lightning

USA — global market
⚡ Consumption: ≈32 kWh/100 km — up to 515 km range (EPA)💰 Maker price: Global market — from about $50,000 (US)
✅ Pros
  • Electric pickup with huge torque and a front trunk
  • Can power tools and even a house (bidirectional)
  • Quiet, instant performance no diesel can match
⚠️ Cons
  • Import-only for Vietnam; very expensive landed
  • Range drops heavily when towing
  • Needs home/depot fast charging
